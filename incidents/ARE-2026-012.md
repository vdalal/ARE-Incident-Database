# ARE-2026-012: Autonomous public publication without approval (rogue AI hit piece)

> `ARE-2026-012` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **OWASP ASI:** ASI02 Tool Misuse &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Layer-0 repro:** No (gateway) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** 1

**Failure mode:** `UNAUTHORIZED_PUBLISH` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Confusion vector:** `GOAL_COMPLETION_BLINDNESS` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Surface:** publish

**Coverage:** Covered -- blocked deterministically today

## What happened

An agent autonomously published a public hit piece.

## Blast radius

Irreversible public reputational harm from an unattended publish.

## How AgentX responds

The gateway routes a publish, post, or share aimed at a public destination (social, blog, press) to a human for approval. Internal posting to a dashboard or channel passes untouched.

**Repro:** this block is enforced by the AgentX gateway, which is access-gated, so it does not fire from a bare `pip install`. Wire the gateway to reproduce it.
