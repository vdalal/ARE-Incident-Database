# ARE-2026-017: Combined multi-agent budget exhaustion on a shared pool (A2A token exhaustion)

> `ARE-2026-017` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **OWASP ASI:** ASI08 Cascading Failures &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Layer-0 repro:** No (gateway) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** 2

**Failure mode:** `COST_EXPLOSION` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Confusion vector:** `COST_EXPLOSION_LOOP` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Surface:** budget-meter

**Coverage:** Covered -- blocked deterministically today

## What happened

A swarm of agents ran away on a shared budget, each staying under its own ceiling.

## Blast radius

Combined runaway spend that no single per-session check sees.

## How AgentX responds

The gateway sums spend across agents that share a budget pool and routes the pool to a human once the combined spend crosses the ceiling.

**Repro:** this block is enforced by the AgentX gateway, which is access-gated, so it does not fire from a bare `pip install`. Wire the gateway to reproduce it.
