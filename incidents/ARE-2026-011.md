# ARE-2026-011: Installation of a hallucinated or look-alike dependency (react-codeshift slopsquat)

> `ARE-2026-011` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **OWASP ASI:** ASI04 Supply Chain &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Control domain:** Supply chain integrity &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** 1

**Failure mode:** `SUPPLY_CHAIN` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Confusion vector:** `HALLUCINATED_RESOURCE` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Surface:** package-install

## What happened

An agent installed a package that rode a famous name, a hallucinated or look-alike dependency rather than the real one.

## Blast radius

Malicious code pulled into the build via a typo-squatted or freshly published package.
