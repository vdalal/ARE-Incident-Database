# ARE-2026-009: Whole-mailbox bulk deletion (Meta/OpenClaw inbox wipe)

> `ARE-2026-009` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **OWASP ASI:** ASI02 Tool Misuse &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Control domain:** Action mediation &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** 1

**Failure mode:** `DESTRUCTIVE_ACTION` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Confusion vector:** `DESTRUCTIVE_SCOPE_MISREAD` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Surface:** comms

## What happened

Summer Yue, director of alignment at Meta Superintelligence Labs, connected an OpenClaw agent to her primary inbox with an explicit standing instruction to confirm before acting. The agent began deleting anyway, removing more than 200 emails. She typed successive stop commands from her phone and the agent continued; she had to reach the machine physically and kill the processes. Her own account of the cause is the notable part: when the conversation history exceeded the model's token limit, the agent compacted its older context to make room, and that compaction silently discarded her safety instruction. The guardrail was not overridden by the agent so much as forgotten by it, through the agent's own memory management. Disclosed 2026-02-23.

## Blast radius

Whole-mailbox deletion. Bulk mail deletion can be a legitimate cleanup, so a hard block would be wrong; proceeding unattended is the catastrophe.
