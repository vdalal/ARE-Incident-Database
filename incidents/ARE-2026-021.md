# ARE-2026-021: Multi-agent conversation loop with runaway spend (GetOnStack $47k/11-day loop)

> `ARE-2026-021` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **OWASP ASI:** ASI08 Cascading Failures &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Coverage class:** Action-coverable &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** 2

**Failure mode:** `COST_EXPLOSION` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Confusion vector:** `COST_EXPLOSION_LOOP` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Surface:** budget-meter

**Coverage class:** Action-coverable -- an inspectable tool call, addressable by deterministic action interception before it runs.

## What happened

A multi-agent conversation looped for eleven days and cost about $47,000.

## Blast radius

Very large runaway spend from an inter-agent loop whose turns are not identical.
