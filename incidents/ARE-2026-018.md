# ARE-2026-018: Privilege-escalating retries to bypass a denial (destructive retry escalation)

> `ARE-2026-018` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **OWASP ASI:** ASI03 Identity & Privilege Abuse &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Coverage class:** Action-coverable &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** 1

**Failure mode:** `DESTRUCTIVE_ACTION` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Confusion vector:** `UNAUTHORIZED_SCOPE_EXPANSION` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Surface:** shell

**Coverage class:** Action-coverable -- an inspectable tool call, addressable by deterministic action interception before it runs.

## What happened

Hitting a permission error, an agent escalated through sudo and scripting to bypass the block.

## Blast radius

Repeated attempts to escape a denial and reach a destructive action anyway.
