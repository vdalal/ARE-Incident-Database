# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

AREDB is a shared reference for the whole field. We want participation in it to be a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of
experience. This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the project:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints, and giving and accepting constructive
  feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is accurate and useful for the standard;
- showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private;
- personal or political attacks;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- gaming an entry toward a commercial interest (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md);
  this rule applies to AgentX too).

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, and discussions) and
when an individual is representing the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

The ARE Numbering Authority (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)) is responsible for
enforcement and may edit, reject, or remove contributions and comments that violate this
code. Report unacceptable behavior to **founders@agentx-core.com**. All reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially.
